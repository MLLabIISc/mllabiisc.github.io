opportunities

Open positions in the Machine Learning Lab.

There are no open positions at the moment. Please check back later. We are unable to respond to part-time, short-term (less than one year) and/or remote student mentorship requests.

The positions below were advertised previously and are kept for reference.

Compression of Neural Networks (Project Associate)

We were looking for candidates for 2 Project Associate positions to work on a rigorous understanding of the compression of neural networks for efficient deployment for server-scale applications, under Professors Govindarajan Ramaswamy and Chiranjib Bhattacharyya. Candidates with an inclination toward research, or who plan to continue in graduate programs, were highly encouraged to apply. Priority was given to candidates with industry experience in either ML or computer systems.

Qualifications:

  • BE/BTech or ME/MTech in CS/EE/ECE or related disciplines
  • Familiarity with deep learning
  • Good understanding of computer architecture
  • Working knowledge of PyTorch

Desired skills:

  • Profiling tools such as µProf and VTune
  • Familiarity with commonly used neural network architectures, such as ResNets, VGGs, Transformers, ViTs
  • Familiarity with model compression, in particular pruning

Applications were sent to efficientml.iisc@gmail.com (deadline Dec. 1, 2023).

Social Robotics (Project Intern / Research Associate)

We were looking for candidates to work on the application of AI in Human-Robot Interaction (HRI): short-term full-time positions in the area of social robotics for special education. For an introduction to the work, see this short project video and the associated publication. Resumes were sent to socialrobotics.mllab@gmail.com.

Project Intern

Responsible for conducting the HRI experiments on the IISc campus: monitoring and operating the robots during the experiments, creating web interfaces for communication with the robot, and storing and annotating the collected data. This is not a remote position. Interns can learn to apply state-of-the-art techniques to create robot behaviour modules spanning vision, speech, and navigation, with opportunities for publication in HRI, field robotics, and AI for Social Good venues.

Desired skillset:

  • B.Tech/BCA in Computer Science or an equivalent discipline
  • Experience with Python or a strong programming background
  • Experience with full-stack website development
  • Good to have: a course or project in robotics, machine learning, computer vision, or speech recognition
  • (Optional) Familiarity with behavioral coding, human-computer interaction

Research Associate

Responsible for designing and implementing complex robot behaviours, such as trajectory planning under various conditions for motor activities with the children; coordinating with the children’s occupational therapists to design tasks with the robot car; and creating state-of-the-art speech recognition models for robust, real-time use by special educators during the HRI study. This is not a remote position.

Desired skillset:

  • B.Tech/M.Tech/PhD in Computer Science or an equivalent discipline
  • Strong foundations in machine learning, deep learning, robotics
  • Experience with computer-vision-based navigation for robotics
  • Experience with Python and OpenCV or an equivalent library
  • Experience with PyTorch/TensorFlow
  • (Optional) Familiarity with speech modeling, ASR, TTS, and end-to-end speech systems
