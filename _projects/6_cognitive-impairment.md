Alzheimer's disease (AD) currently affects about 5.3 million individuals in the United States and more than 30 million worldwide, with a significant increase predicted in the near future. Identifying markers that can affect the disease using machine learning techniques is important for timely treatment. This project, in collaboration with the Centre for Brain Research, studies ML-based prediction of cognitive impairment by understanding brain interactions.
