Has the concept of autonomous vehicles already transformed from fiction to reality? Has the development in this avenue reached a plateau? Not yet — Level 5 autonomy is not even in the picture. The pinnacle will be reached when an autonomous vehicle has the capability to sense and perceive the environment with human accuracy. Simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) is a major component: constructing or updating a map of an unknown environment while simultaneously keeping track of an agent's location within it. Our team, in collaboration with [Ati Motors](https://atimotors.com/), is working on SLAM and occlusion handling.
